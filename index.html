Top 3 Boba Flavors

1. Black Sesame

Why is it my favorite?

The boba itself had a milk base with toasted and crushed black sesame seeds in it.

The best part however was that, unlike any boba (or drink) I've ever had, IT HAD MOCHI IN IT!!

The richness of the sesame milk was complimented by the chewy, plain mochi and it was delicious.

Honorable Mention: Chai Tea

As regular tea, it's rlly good

I haven't had chai boba tea before, but I think I'd love it if I got any.

I've had regular chai tea, however, and IT. IS. SO. PEAK. This is the real Peak Tea.

2. Taro Milk Tea

It's flavor is hard to describe-

But, it's super solid. Its taste is subtle, and

great if you need something on the plainer side!

3. Strawberry Milk Tea

It was from a mall food court...

and suprisingly, it was among the best strawberry milk teas I've ever had.

Strawberry boba is one of my favorite flavors, because it tastes good (who would've guessed)

p.s. the worst boba tea I've had was super bitter because the tea was steeped way too long
